INTERACTIVE DIGITAL WHITEBOARD

Colegio Montano Educational Model | Guatemala CNB Framework

Course & Grade: English | 9th Grade (Recovery)
Teacher: Ms. Karla Quan
Montano Habit: 1. Be Proactive / 2. Begin with the End in Mind
Key Competencies (CNB): Critical Thinking, Communication & Teamwork

Stage I: Motivational Introduction & Initial Challenge

Duration: 8 Minutes
💡 Warm-up Activity / Icebreaker

Activity: "Find Someone Who..."

Students move around the room with a quick checklist to activate prior vocabulary and interact with classmates in English.

📌 Whiteboard Prompts for Students
  • Find someone who likes music.
  • Find someone who plays a sport.
  • Find someone who enjoys reading.
  • Find someone who speaks more than one language.

Stage II: Brief Explanation & Individual Work

Explanation: 12 min | Practice: 30 min
📘 Direct Instruction (Grammar Key)

Basic Sentence Structure: Subject + Verb + Complement

Differentiating complete sentences (full thoughts) from fragments.

Suggested Solution:

Subject = Performs the action | Verb = Action or state | Complement = Additional detailed information.

🎯 CEFR Differentiation (A1 – B1)
A1 Level (Beginner)

Word bank and guided templates: "My name is... I like..." Identify basic subject and verb.

A2 Level (Elementary)

Complete sentences using basic connectors and write 8 original sentences about daily routines.

B1 Level (Intermediate)

Construct short independent paragraphs using subordinating conjunctions and complex sentence structures.

Stage III: Cooperative / Pair & Group Work

Duration: 30 Minutes
👥 Group / Pair Challenge

In pairs, students analyze draft sentences, correct syntax errors, and combine simple sentences to form complex ideas.

Suggested Roles:
  • Reader/Editor: Checks grammar and subject-verb agreement.
  • Speaker/Spokesperson: Presents final results during the plenary.
🤝 Montano Habit in Action

Synergize & Seek First to Understand, Then to Be Understood:

Listen constructively to grammar feedback proposed by team partners before finalizing team outputs.

Stage IV: Plenary, Wrap-up & Formative Assessment

Duration: 10 Minutes
🎤 Plenary Dynamics

Volunteers and selected teams share their constructed sentences on the board. The class collectively identifies Subject, Verb, and Complement, validating corrections in real time.

📊 Plenary Checklist / Assessment Rubric (Colegio Montano)
Evaluation Criterion Max Score Team / Student Teacher Feedback / Notes
1. Topic Mastery & Correct Sentence Structure
2. Clarity in Explanation & Error Correction
3. Collaborative Work & Application of Montano Habits
4. Active Participation & Level-Appropriate Fluency
